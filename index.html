Your health records, on your own machine.

Syntropy Health is an open-source personal health record you run yourself. It connects to your patient portals through the standard SMART on FHIR patient-access APIs, pulls in your wearables, and keeps everything in one private database on hardware you control — no cloud account, no data broker, no ads.

Every provider, one record

Conditions, medications, allergies, labs, vitals, immunizations, visits, procedures, notes and imaging reports from Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks and the VA — merged and de-duplicated.

Wearables alongside labs

Oura, WHOOP and Apple Health (via the iPhone companion app or an export) rolled up by day, next to your clinical trends.

Local-first by design

Records and tokens live only in your instance's database, with tokens encrypted at rest. This website stores nothing and runs no analytics.

How sign-in works

1. Your instance starts a SMART on FHIR sign-in with a PKCE challenge (S256).
2. You sign in on your health system's own patient portal and approve read-only access.
3. The portal redirects to health.syntropylabs.io/callback with a one-time code.
4. That page — static, in your browser — forwards the code to your instance
   (its private address travels in the OAuth "state"; public hosts are refused).
5. Your instance redeems the code with its PKCE verifier and downloads records directly.

The code is useless without the verifier, which never leaves your instance. Technical details →
