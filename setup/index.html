Get started

Your whole health record, on a computer you own.

Syntropy Health brings your patient-portal records, lab results, Apple Health, Oura and WHOOP into one private app that runs at home. Setup takes about 15 minutes, and the iPhone app connects in a few taps.

What you get

Every record in one place

Conditions, medications, allergies, labs, visits, immunizations and notes from Epic MyChart, Oracle Health, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks and the VA, merged and de-duplicated.

Wearables next to your labs

Sleep, heart rate, HRV, workouts and more from Apple Health, Oura and WHOOP, shown day by day alongside your clinical trends, with overlapping devices counted once.

Ask your own AI

Get answers and coaching from your data with your own Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter key, or connect Claude Desktop and other agents directly. Read-only, and every lookup is logged.

Private by design

Everything lives in one database on your computer, with sign-in tokens encrypted. No cloud account, no analytics, and nothing sold or shared.

Choose where it runs

Syntropy Health is a small app that runs in the background and opens in your browser. Pick the computer that suits you; you can move later with a backup.

Set it up

  1. Install Docker Desktop

    Docker runs Syntropy Health in its own sealed box, so it doesn't change anything else on your computer. Download Docker Desktop, install it and open it once. On a home server, install Docker Engine and the Compose plugin instead.

  2. Download Syntropy Health

    Download the ZIP

    Unzip it, rename the folder to syntropy-health and move it to your home folder. Keep it there: your data is saved inside it, in the data folder.

    Prefer Git? 
    git clone https://github.com/EnriqueNeyra/syntropy-health ~/syntropy-health

    Updating is then a git pull away.

  3. Start it

    Open Terminal (Mac: press ⌘ Space and type Terminal) or PowerShell (Windows: Start menu), then run:

    Mac, Linux, home server 
    cd ~/syntropy-health
docker compose up -d
    Windows 
    cd $HOME\syntropy-health
docker compose up -d

    The first start takes a few minutes while it builds. After that it starts on its own whenever Docker does. If your computer asks whether to allow incoming connections, choose Allow so your iPhone can reach it.

  4. Open it in your browser

    Go to http://localhost:8000, enter your name and choose a passphrase. Add family members as separate profiles whenever you like.

  5. Connect your iPhone

    In the Syntropy Health iPhone app, open Settings → Connect to my server and enter your computer's address and your passphrase. You can also pair with a code from Sources → iPhone & Apple Watch → Pair iPhone.

    The address is your computer's address on your home Wi-Fi, for example http://192.168.1.20:8000. On a Mac it's in System Settings → Wi-Fi → Details. Once connected, the whole app appears in the iPhone app's Syntropy tab and your Health data syncs in the background.

  6. Add your records

    Open Sources → Connect a health system, find your hospital or clinic and sign in with your patient-portal login. Connect Oura or WHOOP the same way, or type in lab results from a report.

Using it away from home

Syntropy Health doesn't send your data through our servers, so reaching it from outside your home is up to you. The simplest private option is Tailscale: install it on your computer and iPhone with the same account, then add your computer's Tailscale address in the iPhone app under Settings → Addresses.

Avoid opening port 8000 to the internet. If you do make it reachable publicly, keep a strong passphrase on.

Bring your own AI

In Settings → AI & agents, paste an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter to use Ask and to read lab reports from photos or PDFs. Claude Desktop, Claude Code and other MCP apps can also connect directly; the same page shows the exact setup.

Your AI provider receives your questions and only the data it looks up.

Questions

Does my health data go to Syntropy?

No. Records download straight from your health system to your computer, and your iPhone sends Health data straight to it too. Patient-portal sign-in passes a one-time code through a static page on this site, in your browser; Oura and WHOOP sign-in uses a small relay because those services require a secret. Neither sees your health data. How it works

What happens when my computer is asleep?

Nothing is lost. Your iPhone keeps new Health data and sends it when the computer is awake again, and scheduled record and wearable syncs catch up. For round-the-clock syncing, run it on an always-on machine.

Does it cost anything?

No. Syntropy Health is free and open source. If you use Ask, your AI provider bills you directly for your own key.

How do I update?

Download the new ZIP and replace everything in your syntropy-health folder except the data folder (or run git pull), then run docker compose up -d --build in that folder.

How do I back up my data?

In the app, open Settings → Your data → Download database backup, or copy the data folder. The backup restores everything on a new computer.

Can I use the iPhone app without a server?

Yes. The app browses, charts and exports your Apple Health data on its own, and can send it on a schedule to a webhook, Home Assistant, MQTT or a cloud folder. Connect a server whenever you're ready.

Is it a replacement for my doctor or patient portal?

No. It's a personal copy of your records for understanding and sharing them. Always check anything important with your care team. See the terms.
